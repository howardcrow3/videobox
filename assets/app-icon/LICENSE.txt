VideoBox app icon (videobox.svg and every file derived from it in this directory:
videobox-small.svg, videobox-1024.png, videobox-macos-512.png, videobox.icns, videobox.ico,
hicolor/, and apps/videobox-web/web/favicon.png).

The icon is the Turtle Box turtle mark set on a rounded ink tile. It is a Turtle Box brand mark, not
open source, and is not covered by the project's MIT OR Apache-2.0 license. Its terms are in
docs/brand/LICENSE-brand.txt: it may be used unmodified as part of VideoBox, and forks and modified
versions must replace it with their own icon.
